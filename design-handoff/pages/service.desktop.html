Signs you need a repair

Hearing something new? Bring it in.

Small problems get expensive when they wait. Any of these is worth a call.

Schedule service

Strange noises

Grinding, squealing or knocking usually means a motor, brake or suspension part is on its way out.

Slow performance

Losing speed points to the engine, batteries or electrical system.

Battery problems

Not holding a charge or dying quickly. Often a battery, sometimes a charger.

Brakes not right

Noise when stopping or a soft pedal. Do not put this one off.

Rough or bumpy ride

Vibration or pulling means suspension, alignment or worn tires.

Lights flickering

Headlights or taillights cutting out is an electrical issue.

Burning smells

A sign of overheating or an electrical fault. Stop driving and call.

Hard to start

Slow to start or will not start at all. Usually ignition or starter.

Good questions

Before you book.

Do not see yours? Call (352) 307-0111 and ask for the shop.

When should I bring my cart in?

As soon as you notice something new: a noise, a slow start, a battery that will not hold a charge. Early repairs are cheaper repairs.

What carts do you work on?

All major gas and electric carts, including Club Car, Yamaha and E-Z-GO.

Do you pick up?

Yes. Our driver can collect your cart and bring it back when the work is done. Ask when you schedule.

How long will it take?

Most repairs are finished within 24 hours of your approval. Your estimate will tell you if yours needs longer.

Do you do annual service?

Yes. An annual service covers the items that keep a cart reliable and safe for another season.

Can you convert my cart to lithium?

Yes. We install Bolt Energy lithium packs in 36, 48 and 72 volt carts, with a 12 year warranty.
