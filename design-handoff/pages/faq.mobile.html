Rentals

Rentals

Is there a minimum rental period?

Yes, 3 days. After that, rent by the week, the month or the season.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery and pickup to your home, rental or venue across Marion, Sumter, Lake and Alachua counties. Event fleets travel statewide.

Are the rental carts gas or electric?

Gas. There is nothing to plug in and they run all day.

Can I extend my rental?

Call before your end date and we will extend it if the cart is available.

How many carts can you supply for an event?

A few or a few hundred. The fleet is over 300 carts.
