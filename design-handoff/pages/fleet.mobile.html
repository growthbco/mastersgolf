Masters Golf Cars is the source in Ocala, The Villages, Central Florida and the Southeast for golf cart fleet rentals. We keep a rental fleet of over 300 carts for large events and can supply carts anywhere in the Southeast.

All fleet rentals are late model, gas powered carts in 2 passenger, 4 passenger, 6 passenger and utility. Daily, weekly and monthly rates, with discounted large event and seasonal lease rates. 3 day minimum.
